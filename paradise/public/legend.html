Understanding the signals

  • Quiet means carry on: after Arrived you're there, and during a room scan it means hold still while the camera looks.
  • Room scan (finding something that isn't in view): turn pulses take you round a full circle in 10 steps, with a quiet stop at each.
  • Stop beats everything else, in every mode, except while reaching for the thing.
  • Voice only listens to what starts with “Paradise” (“Paradise, take me to the Waymo”), so people talking nearby change nothing. Every answer is a buzz: Heard you while it works it out, then Got it, Stopped, or Didn't understand.
  • Guidance waits until Got it is over, so it can be counted.
  • How strong every buzz is follows Buzz strength in the laptop page's settings.
