Every buzz, and what it means

Three signals: the left or right Joy-Con held on means rotate that way, both held on means go forward, and a double buzz means you've reached it.

Press play on any buzz to watch its rhythm on the two wrists pinned below. With the laptop page open in this browser and no mode running, you feel it on the Joy-Cons too.

Understanding the signals

  • Quiet means don't walk: stand still. That's while it looks for the thing, when something is in the way, and after Reached.
  • Looking round (the thing isn't in view): rotate pulses turn you round the room a step at a time, with a quiet stop at each while the camera looks.
  • If the thing drops out of view, the rotate buzz turns you back to where it was.
  • Close to it, it goes quiet for a moment while the AI checks a photo: then either Reached, or a short forward buzz (one more step) and it checks again.
  • How strong every buzz is follows Buzz strength in the laptop page's settings.
